Local mobility

The velocity ellipse maps unit-norm joint rates in radians per second through the position Jacobian. Its drawing is normalized to a fixed major axis; its shape shows directional imbalance, not an absolute speed scale. Joint-limit restrictions are not included in this unconstrained differential diagnostic.

CCD visits joints from tip to base. Each update minimizes tip distance over that joint’s permitted interval with other angles fixed. Ten sweeps with improvement at most 10⁻¹⁰ units report a plateau; this does not prove the target is impossible. The radial reach annulus ignores joint limits. A tolerance hit is a position result, not a collision-free motion plan.
